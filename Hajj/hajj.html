Hajj from Japan

What you need to prepare, and the platforms and agencies that can help you register and travel.

Hajj rules, quotas, and registration steps are set by Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah and can change every year. Always confirm current requirements on the official Nusuk platform before booking anything.

How the process generally works

1

Register on Nusuk Hajj

Japan is one of the Muslim-minority countries served through Saudi Arabia's official Nusuk Hajj platform. You create a pilgrim profile there with your passport details before anything else.

2

Get verified

Nusuk checks your documents and marks your profile as a "Verified Pilgrim." This makes you eligible to select a package once bookings open.

3

Choose a package

Packages are sold directly through Nusuk by authorized providers and typically bundle visa, flights, accommodation in Makkah/Madinah, meals, and transport. You can also book through a Japan-based agency that assists with the Nusuk process.

4

Prepare documents and health requirements

Finalize your passport, vaccination certificate, and any other paperwork before departure (see checklist below).
