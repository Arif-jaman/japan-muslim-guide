Muslim Graveyards

Islamic burial sites in Japan, and the organizations that help arrange Janazah (funeral) and burial.

Japan has very few burial grounds that accept Islamic burial — most sources cite around seven sites nationwide, concentrated mainly in the Kanto region. If you are arranging a burial, please contact the organizations below as early as possible; in Islam, burial is meant to take place as soon as possible after death.
